# Rules for the 36 collection directories

English edition, 2026-10-27. Category definitions translate the 2026-10-20 confirmed preferences and suggested defaults. Actual directory names remain Chinese.

**Confirmed** means explicitly chosen by the user. **Suggested** means a working definition, not an established user preference. **Paused** means the collection has a definition but is outside current routing scope. Report new, renamed, or split directories rather than silently mapping them to similar names.

## Resolving overlap

1. Identify the resource: feature, anthology, documentary, performance, episode, extra, multipart film, or series. Resolve uncertain identities first; keep associated resources together.
2. Exclusions, pauses, and explicit title-specific decisions take precedence. 华语世界 and series do not participate in current destination selection.
3. 名著改编 is the confirmed priority collection entry, ahead of genre. Verify adaptation from a significant literary work. Inspiration, an allusion, or a shared title is insufficient. Research uncertain literary status before raising a preference question.
4. There is no confirmed global ranking among the other directories. Judge the work's overall structure, perspective, themes, and style rather than website tag order. Do not invent rules such as religion always outranking children.
5. Confirmed local preferences: family-centered narratives belong in 家庭; character change driven by a journey structure belongs in 公路; poverty, war, or social suffering through a child's perspective belongs in 儿童; strongly social romances do not belong in 爱情; 宗教 includes religious protagonists and works centered on religious symbols.
6. Where confirmed rules genuinely conflict, select a primary location if the evidence and user preferences support it with at least approximately 80% confidence within the authorized inbox task. Otherwise hold it for one concrete preference decision. The explicit 名著改编 priority still applies.
7. If identity is established but no category dominates a narrative about life, society, and psychology, use 故事. Unidentified films remain pending research.

## Directory definitions

| Directory | Status | Include | Boundaries |
|---|---|---|---|
| 故事 | Confirmed | Character circumstances, lived experience, social observation, and psychological change without another dominant category; strongly social romantic dramas may fit. | Not every Drama tag, and never a substitute for identifying a film. Apply clear confirmed family/child rules. |
| 家庭 | Confirmed | Serious family drama centered on marriage, parenthood, generations, household structure, or living together; family-centered postwar trauma. | Having relatives or a family argument is insufficient; romance is not automatically family drama. |
| 公路 | Confirmed | A sustained journey organizes encounters, relationships, and character transformation. | Vehicles, business trips, or an initial relocation are insufficient. Migration and escape can qualify if the journey structure truly drives the film. |
| 爱情 | Confirmed | Formation, development, loss, separation, or reunion of romantic relationships organizes the film, without a strongly social focus. | Even romance-led films are excluded when class, labor, survival, or institutions are strongly central; consider 故事 or another clear category. |
| 青春 | Confirmed | Coming of age, identity formation, and conflict with the adult world, including violent or oppressive subjects. | Young actors alone are insufficient. Transition toward adulthood suggests 青春; childhood experience suggests 儿童. Research unclear ages. |
| 儿童 | Confirmed | Works children can understand, and life or growth through a child's perspective, including poverty, war, and social suffering. | Suitability for children is not the only criterion. A supporting child does not qualify an adult-centered film. Recognize overlap with animation. |
| 政法委 | Confirmed | Institutions, justice, political power, or investigative journalism run through the film; political espionage when power and institutions organize it. | Social injustice or class as background is insufficient; a judge's family story does not qualify by profession alone. |
| 传记 | Confirmed | An identifiable real person's experiences and identity are central. | True-event inspiration, fictional composites, or ordinary social circumstances alone are insufficient. |
| 一段历史 | Confirmed | A public event and its development or operation are central. | Period setting or factual inspiration alone is insufficient; a person's life as the focus suggests 传记. |
| 名著改编 | Confirmed | Verified adaptations of significant literary works; takes priority over genre. | Not every novel adaptation qualifies. Original screenplays later published, loose inspiration, and shared titles are insufficient. |
| 战争 | Confirmed | War experience, military action, or military systems dominate. | Wartime background alone is insufficient. Family-centered aftermath goes to 家庭; romance follows 爱情 rules; growth may fit 故事/青春/儿童. Child-perspective war follows 儿童. |
| 宗教 | Confirmed | Faith, religious institutions, and individual choice; religious protagonists such as priests or nuns, or narratives centered on religious symbols. | A passing church, religious word, or minor character is insufficient. A religious protagonist need not be engaged in a primarily theological plot. |
| 体育 | Suggested | Training, competition, sporting events, or athletic careers substantially organize the narrative. | A sporting profession or occasional game is insufficient; flag equally central family/growth themes. Single-title approvals do not confirm a universal rule. |
| 音乐 | Suggested | Creation, rehearsal, recording, performance, and musical practice are central; standalone concert recordings are candidates. | A good score or a musician in a romance is insufficient. Do not assume priority over 纪录 or 传记. |
| 歌舞 | Suggested | Singing and dancing carry narrative and emotional expression as the principal musical-film form. | A music profession, concert excerpt, or end-credit dance is insufficient. Concert recordings are not automatically musicals. |
| 喜剧 | Suggested | Comic situations, misunderstanding, satire, or absurdity continuously organize the story and intended experience. | Occasional jokes, dark humor, or bittersweet wit are insufficient; social drama may remain 故事. |
| 犯罪 | Suggested | Crime, criminal careers, investigation, gangs, or the causal consequences of crime organize the film. | Crime as background trauma is insufficient. Institutional justice may fit 政法委; sustained danger may fit 惊悚. |
| 惊悚 | Suggested | Sustained threat, escalation, pursuit, psychological manipulation, or suspense organizes the experience. | Fast pacing, job pressure, a murder, or disturbing drama alone is insufficient. |
| 恐怖 | Suggested | Fear, shock, uncanniness, or bodily/supernatural threat is the central experience and mechanism. | Violence, death, mental illness, religion, or misery alone is insufficient. Consider 怪兽 where appropriate. |
| 动作 | Suggested | Combat, pursuit, martial arts, stunts, and physical confrontation continuously drive the narrative. | A few fights are insufficient. Sports are not action by default; military-centered work may fit 战争. Series remain paused. |
| 冒险 | Suggested | Exploration, treasure hunting, missions, expeditions, or successive obstacles organize the action. | Ordinary travel is insufficient. Character transformation through travel suggests 公路; disaster survival suggests 灾难. |
| 灾难 | Suggested | Large accidents, natural disasters, or catastrophe, including warning, escape, rescue, and survival, dominate. | Personal misfortune or a disaster only in the backstory is insufficient. Aftermath centered on family/life follows 家庭/故事. |
| 怪兽 | Suggested | Monsters or giant creatures, their attacks, opposition, or relationships organize the film. | Ordinary animal drama or a brief creature appearance is insufficient. Explain overlaps with horror/science fiction without inventing priority. |
| 奇幻 | Suggested | Magic, myth, supernatural forces, or nonrealistic rules are indispensable to the narrative. | Poetic images, brief dreams, and symbolism alone are insufficient. Recognize confirmed religious criteria when applicable. |
| 科幻 | Suggested | Speculative technology, aliens, futures, or scientific premises supply indispensable world rules and story mechanisms. | Real-world science, medicine, and space news are not science fiction by themselves. Spectacle is not required. |
| 西部 | Suggested | Frontier order, settlement, cowboys/gunfighters, and Western cultural or genre traditions organize the work. | Western US locations, deserts, or horses alone are insufficient. Nontraditional settings require genre evidence. |
| 黑色电影 | Suggested | Supported film-noir or neo-noir tradition combining crime/moral conflict with fatalism, ambiguity, corruption, and style. | Black-and-white images, darkness, tragic endings, or crime tags alone are insufficient. Do not decide by color or date. |
| 情色 | Suggested | Desire, erotic expression, or exploration of sexual relationships is central to the experience. | Nudity, sex-worker characters, or a suggestive title is insufficient. Social relationships or trauma may point to 故事. |
| CULT | Suggested | Documented cult reception, distinctive fan culture, or sustained subcultural circulation makes this collection entry meaningful. | Obscurity, taboo, low budget, sex, violence, or oddness alone is insufficient. Never a miscellaneous fallback. |
| 实验电影 | Suggested | Experiment with image, sound, time, structure, or viewing takes precedence and substantially challenges conventional narrative. | Slowness, long takes, poetry, obscurity, or difficulty alone is insufficient. Distinguish art-house drama from experimental film. |
| 纪录 | Suggested | Nonfiction recording or examination of real people, events, or processes is the principal form. | Dramatized true stories are not documentaries. Reenactments do not automatically disqualify one; research hybrids. Disc extras remain attached. |
| 动画片 | Suggested | Animation is the principal production and expressive form of a standalone work. | Not synonymous with children's films. Brief animation or extensive live-action effects are insufficient. Adult animation can qualify. |
| 动漫改真 | Suggested | Verified live-action adaptation of manga/comics or animation, as a source-medium entry. | Not animation itself, comic-like style, game adaptations, or superpowers alone. Priority relative to other categories is unconfirmed. |
| 短篇集 | Suggested | Distinct independent shorts form an anthology, or a short-film collection is explicitly collected as a whole. | Chapters, multiple plotlines, CD parts, and ordinary franchises are not automatically anthologies. Keep the group intact. |
| 华语世界 | Paused; structure confirmed | The user's focused collection of Chinese-language films or films by Chinese creators, internally arranged by region and selected directors. | Not all Asian films, nor any film with a Chinese supporting actor. No current routing or incidental regional/director reorganization. |
| 合集 | Paused; structure confirmed | Deliberately collected series/themes, potentially spanning directors, animation, features, and TV. 要分类 is an intake area, not a genre. | Shared director or genre does not authorize a new collection. Identify series and preserve associations without reorganizing them. |

## Decisions and authorization

Earlier acceptance of specific Story-review recommendations applies to those titles, not all suggested definitions or unrelated batches. Consult the actual report, current files, and later instructions. New rules may change old candidates; explain the change.

The current inbox authorization permits direct moves at approximately 80% confidence with established identity and successful safety checks. It does not authorize deletions, duplicate replacement, or series organization. Keep individual PR decisions as title-specific precedents unless the user states a general preference.
